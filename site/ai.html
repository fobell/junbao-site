04 · METHOD

按拍电影的顺序做 AIAI, in the orderfilms are made

多数人一上来就写提示词。我先把片子想清楚，模型只负责最后一步。Most people start by typing prompts. I work the film out first; the model only does the last step.

  1. 创意Idea

    一句话说清楚这个片子讲什么、给谁看。One sentence on what the film is about and who it's for.

  2. 故事大纲与剧本Outline & script

    起承转合定下来，再写成能拍的剧本。Fix the arc, then write a shootable script.

  3. 分镜Storyboard

    每个镜头的景别、运镜、时长先排好。Plan framing, camera move and length for every shot.

  4. 角色、场景、道具设定Characters, sets, props

    先做设定图，保证每个镜头里的人和物是同一个。Design sheets first, so people and objects stay consistent across shots.

    用到：uses: prompt.junbao.ai ↗

  5. 镜头生成Shot generation

    按分镜逐个生成镜头，挑最好的那一条。Generate shot by shot from the storyboard and keep the best take.

    Seedance · Higgsfield · 即梦Jimeng

  6. 剪辑与声音Edit & sound

    能在剪辑里解决的，不重新生成。配音、音乐、字幕最后统一。Fix it in the edit before regenerating. Voice, music and captions come together last.
